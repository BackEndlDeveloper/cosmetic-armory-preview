# Aurelian Armory – Expanded Cosmetic Previews for Darktide

Welcome to **Aurelian Armory**, a community-crafted extension for Warhammer 40,000: Darktide that reimagines how you interact with the Commodore’s Vestures store. Instead of simply browsing static thumbnails, this mod transforms your shopping experience into a living, breathing armory showcase—letting you test-drive armor, helmets, and accessories on any of your veteran operatives before you commit a single Requisition point. Think of it as the difference between reading a menu and sitting down at the feast.

The core philosophy behind Aurelian Armory is simple: **your character’s identity should never be a gamble**. In the grim darkness of the far future, there is only war—and equally, there is only one chance to make the right aesthetic choice. This mod peels back the curtain of uncertainty, giving you a full, real-time preview of how each cosmetic piece interacts with your existing wardrobe, skin tones, scars, and even the lighting of the Mourningstar. It is a tool for the discerning Reject who believes that even the most brutal combat deserves a touch of personal flair.

Built with modularity and performance in mind, Aurelian Armory does not reskin or alter the base game files. Instead, it operates as an overlay, injecting a suite of intuitive preview panels and drag-and-drop comparison tools directly into the Vestures interface. Whether you are a veteran player with hundreds of hours or a fresh face just stepping off the shuttle, the mod’s design philosophy prioritizes clarity, speed, and the sheer joy of discovery. Let us walk through the armory gates together.

## 🎯 Why Aurelian Armory Exists

The Commodore’s Vestures, while vast and ever-rotating, has always suffered from a peculiar blindness: it shows you the armor, but not the wearer. You see the helmet, but not how it sits atop your particular forehead scar. You see the pauldron, but not how it clashes with your favorite torso piece. This disconnect forces players to rely on memory, external wikis, or risky blind purchases—a plague upon the meticulous fashion-focused Reject.

Aurelian Armory was born from a simple observation: **previews should be as detailed as the purchase**. This project serves as an answer to the question, “What if the store felt like a true fitting room?” By integrating a dynamic preview canvas that supports all four playable classes, the mod ensures that every display is honest, every texture is rendered in real-time, and every combination is testable. It is not just a utility; it is a statement that player agency extends beyond the battlefield and into the void of cosmetic self-expression.

Furthermore, this mod addresses the complexity of layered armor pieces. Darktide’s cosmetic system allows for head, torso, and leg combinations, but the absence of a unified inspection tool often leads to mismatched hues or clipping issues. Aurelian Armory features a **"Thread Inspector"** that highlights potential collision zones between pieces, uses color-variance indicators to show materials that clash, and provides a 360-degree rotation view with adjustable lighting to simulate the fog of Tertium or the harsh glow of the ship’s engines. This level of detail encourages experimentation and rewards players who seek cohesion in their warband’s appearance.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🛡️ Full-Length Character Preview
Unlike the base store which only shows a rigid front-facing pose, Aurelian Armory places your operative in a neutral, interactive stance. You can rotate the camera 360-degrees, zoom in to inspect the intricate stitches on a flak jacket, and zoom out to see how the entire silhouette changes your profile on the battlefield. This feature supports all four classes (Veteran, Zealot, Psyker, and Ogryn), ensuring accuracy across body types and proportions.

### 🧩 Modular Piece Comparison
Have your heart set on a specific helmet but want to see it with three different chest pieces? The mod introduces a **"Sidereal Side-by-Side"** mode. This splits your screen into up to four independent preview windows, each rendering a different configuration simultaneously. You can lock a baseline armor setup and cycle through new items in the additional panes, allowing for instant visual A/B testing without repetitive menu navigation.

### 🎨 Texture & Material Tint Analyzer
Fabrics, metals, and leathers behave differently under light. The built-in analyzer charts the primary and secondary color values of any selected item and displays them against the palette of your currently equipped gear. An overlapping heatmap shows where colors are complementary (green) and where they clash (red). This data-driven approach empowers players to make informed decisions, eliminating the guesswork of whether that crimson sash will look harmonious with your bronze shoulder plates.

### 🧠 Persistent Visual Cache
Previous previews and combinations are saved locally in a lightweight cache. If you revisit a section of the store, your last examined loadouts are restored instantly. This feature accelerates the decision-making process, allowing for continuous comparison across days without re-rendering assets. The cache is automatically purged upon new game patches to prevent conflicts.

### 🌍 Multilingual Interface
Recognizing the global nature of the Mourningstar’s crew, Aurelian Armory ships with localization files for High Gothic (English), Low Gothic (German), and several other Imperial dialects. The interface dynamically adapts to your game’s language settings, ensuring that terms like "Preview" and "Compare" are always presented in your native tongue. Community contributors are actively adding more languages through a transparent string system.

### 📖 Immersive Lore Tooltips
Every cosmetic item now carries a contextual lore snippet when browsed through the preview mode. These snippets, written in the flavor of the Imperium, describe the item’s likely origin, the forge world it came from, or the regiment it once served. This addition deepens the narrative immersion and turns every shopping trip into a small piece of storytelling.

## 🛠️ System Requirements & Integration

Aurelian Armory is designed to harmonize with the current version of Darktide. It requires a functional mod loader compatible with the game’s runtime environment. The mod is built as a standalone module and does not require additional dependencies beyond the standard user interface framework.

- **Memory Footprint**: Approximately 20 MB of additional RAM when previews are active.
- **Frame Rate Impact**: Minimal; rendering is paused when the preview window is minimized.
- **Compatibility**: Fully compatible with the base game and the official Commodore’s Vestures store. It does not modify any game data, ensuring fair-play integrity and preventing conflicts with anti-cheat mechanisms.

## 🖥️ Responsive User Interface

The interface of Aurelian Armory is built with a responsive design philosophy, akin to a well-forged chainblade—every link is purposeful. Whether you are playing on a slender 16:9 monitor or an ultrawide 32:9 display, the preview panels scale and reposition fluidly to ensure no buttons are obscured. The UI also supports custom scaling factors for those who prefer smaller or larger font sizes.

On a technical level, the UI uses a hybrid rendering approach that combines rasterized textures for the background with vector graphics for the interactive elements. This results in crisp outlines for selection boxes and smooth glowing edges for active previews, even at high zoom levels. Keyboard shortcuts are fully rebindable, allowing you to cycle through preview modes, toggle the tint analyzer, or capture a screenshot of your current loadout for sharing with your warband.

## 🌙 Nocturnal Mode & Accessibility

The Mourningstar has low-light environments, and so does your desktop. Aurelian Armory includes a dedicated **Nocturnal Mode** that shifts the UI palette to darker tones, reducing eye strain during extended browsing sessions. This mode also increases the contrast of selection highlights and enlarges tooltips for better readability.

Accessibility was a core consideration in the design. Color-blind filters are built into the tint analyzer, offering Protanopia, Deuteranopia, and Tritanopia correction profiles. The interface supports full keyboard navigation, and all text elements are readable by standard screen-reading software. We believe that every Reject deserves the right to see their armor clearly, regardless of physical limitations.

## 🛠️ Configuration & Customization

Upon first launch, Aurelian Armory generates a configuration file that allows deep customization of the mod’s behavior. Here, you can adjust the default zoom level, toggle the automatic cache saving, bind specific keys for the comparison mode, and even set a "favorite loadout" that appears as a persistent watermark in the corner of your screen.

Advanced users can write custom Lua-style scripts to define new sorting rules for the store. For example, you could create a rule that sorts all items by their primary color value, or one that groups all items from a specific forge world together. These scripts are executed in a sandboxed environment that cannot access the game’s core processes, ensuring safety and stability.

## ⚙️ Troubleshooting & Community Support

Should you encounter anomalies, the mod includes a built-in diagnostic console. This console logs all UI actions and rendering times, identifying potential bottlenecks or conflicts with other mods. The log file is automatically saved to your local profile folder, and you can copy-paste its contents directly into a community support thread for rapid diagnosis.

- **Symptom**: Previews show a blank screen. **Solution**: Clear the persistent visual cache via the mod’s main menu and restart the game.
- **Symptom**: UI elements overlap. **Solution**: Reset the UI scale factor to 100% and disable any custom window scaling from your operating system.
- **Symptom**: Tint analyzer shows no data. **Solution**: Ensure your graphics settings are set to at least the "Medium" texture detail level.
